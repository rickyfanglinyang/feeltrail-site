Privacy Policy (English)

FeelTrail is a local-first personal body-condition logging app.

What This Means

  • No account is required.
  • No backend is used.
  • No cloud sync is used.
  • No analytics are used.
  • No ads are shown.
  • No third-party tracking is added.

Data Handling

  • Body-condition logs stay on your device unless you export CSV.
  • You can delete all local logs from the app.
  • Exported CSV files are created only when you choose to export them.

Medical Scope

  • FeelTrail is not a diagnosis tool.
  • FeelTrail is not a treatment tool.
  • FeelTrail is not a medical advice tool.

FeelTrail helps users record and summarize personal body condition logs. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. If a user feels seriously unwell, they should contact a qualified medical professional or local emergency service.
